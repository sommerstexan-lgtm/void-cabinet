How to play. Click the deck (dark stack, top left) to turn cards. Click a card, then click where it should go — or drag it. Double-click a card to send it to a foundation if it fits.
Columns: stack cards high to low, red on black or black on red. Only a King can start an empty column. Foundations (four A piles): build Ace through King of the same suit.
